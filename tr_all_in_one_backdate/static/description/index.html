All In One Backdate

by Technical Rajni — Solutions | Automation | Innovation

Confirm any document with a custom past date

Assign a backdate to Sale Orders, Purchase Orders, Invoices, Payments and Inventory Transfers. Backdate propagates to stock moves and journal entries automatically.

Supported Documents

Sale Order

Confirm sales with a custom order date. Propagates to delivery and stock moves.

Purchase Order

Confirm purchases with a past date. Updates delivery and stock moves.

Invoice / Bill

Post invoices and bills with a custom invoice date and accounting date.

Payment

Register payments with a past date. Updates journal entries.

Inventory Transfer

Validate transfers with a past date. Updates stock moves and product moves.

Key Features

  • Set backdate on individual records or mass assign from list view
  • Add remarks/reason with every backdate
  • Make remarks mandatory from Settings
  • Enable/disable each module independently
  • Backdate logged in chatter for audit trail
  • Works with multi-company

Configuration

Go to Settings → Backdate and enable the features you need.
